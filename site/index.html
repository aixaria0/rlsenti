01 / INGEST

Reality enters as an event, not a conclusion.

Capability, peer, room, lemma and source context arrive before interpretation. The compiler keeps the origin visible.

02 / NORMALIZE

Shape is made stable before meaning is promoted.

Protocol-specific objects become deterministic typed structures. Naming drift and source shape stay at the adapter boundary.

03 / COMPILE

Execution becomes a reproducible derivation.

Rholang subset reduction, exchange state, QLF-oriented data and block-like evidence are derived from a pinned scenario.

04 / ENVELOPE

Every artifact carries its parent.

Payload hash, previous envelope hash, source fields and verification state are sealed into a linked provenance chain.

05 / REPLAY

Counterfactuals are useful only when divergence is localizable.

Mutate one fact, recompile the world, and preserve the first layer where the evidence chain changes.

06 / WITNESS

The verdict ends where the evidence ends.

PASS, WARN and FAIL carry a concrete basis. Missing authority is reported as a boundary instead of being silently promoted.
